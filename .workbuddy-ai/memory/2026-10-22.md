## Evidence, and what it forced

Probe on the shipped asset: **18/18 clips clean**, `takeoff→cruise` 0 bones > 8°, `land→idle` 0.
Then the three historical defects were re-injected into the real rig: `idle` neck raise →
4 anomalies (horizon −60.0°, Neck2 −28.2°, Neck1 −24.7°, Head −21.1°); `walk` jaw +8.6° → 1 (8.7°);
`land` Neck2 +15.9° → 1 (11.3° at `land → idle`).

Three decisions the measurements forced:

- **Median, not peak.** A wingbeat swings 60° for a few frames; a postural defect is held. A peak
  envelope flags every healthy flight clip.
- **Postural chain only** (`Body, Spine1..3, Neck1..2, Head`). Distal wing/digit bones legitimately
  hold >18° in every flight clip.
- **`CANONICAL_TRANSITIONS` is only `takeoff→cruise` and `land→idle`.** Measured on the healthy
  asset: `cruise→bankLeft/bankRight` differ by 8 bones (worst elbow 39.9°), `idle↔walk` by 22
  (worst `L_Toes` 44.2°). Those are loop-to-loop and crossfaded by `runtime.ts`, so the delta is a
  phase, not a snap — including them makes the detector permanently red.

## The bug found on the way (affects the existing `Scan`)

**Writing a bone rotation and then calling `mixer.update(0)` on a paused action leaves that action
unable to write the pose again.** Reproduced minimally: reset `bone.quaternion`, `mixer.update(0)`,
then seek again → the bone reads untouched, forever. `scanSeam` borrows the playback mixer, so a
**second `Scan` press in one session measures a stale pose and reports the clip clean**. Measured:
first `sampleClipPoses` −21.1°, every later call identity.

Fix used here: `sampleClipPoses` runs a **private `AnimationMixer`**, and reports bind values for
bones the clip does not animate (rather than resetting the scene, which is what poisons the mixer).
Regression test asserts a repeated scan reproduces the first result. **`scanSeam` still has the
defect** — left alone as out of scope.
